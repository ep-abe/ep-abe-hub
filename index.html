Common questions

Click any question below to expand the answer

How do I register for courses?
Check the Academic Calendar to confirm registration is open. Then sign in to SIS with your JHED ID and use Registration / My Cart.
What important dates do I need to know?
Use the official Academic Calendar for registration windows, first day of classes, tuition deadlines, drop/refund dates, and graduation application deadlines.
How do I apply to graduate?
Visit the Graduation Information page for application instructions and WSE master’s commencement details.
Where is the rest of the advising information?
Open the FAQs page for degree rules, program-specific guidance, and the Forms & Contacts page for the right person to email.
Need help? Contact the Student Academic Success Office at ep-studentsuccess@jhu.edu. Program contacts are listed on the Applied Biomedical Engineering program page.
