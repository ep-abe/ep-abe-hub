ABE students must select one of eight focus areas. The focus area does not appear as an official designation on the transcript. Full course lists are on the Courses page.

Artificial Intelligence in Medicine. Apply AI, machine learning, and related computational methods to diagnosis, treatment planning, predictive analytics, brain-computer interfaces, and healthcare outcomes.

Biomechanics. Model and analyze mechanical function in biological systems, from cellular forces to human movement.

Biomedical Data Science. Analyze and model large-scale biomedical datasets using statistics, analytics, computational modeling, and bioinformatics.

Clinical Engineering. Integrate engineering with healthcare systems and patient care, including rehabilitation engineering and regulatory considerations.

Imaging. Study biomedical imaging and image processing for medical diagnosis and research.

Medical Devices. Design, develop, and evaluate biomedical sensors and instruments.

Neuroengineering. Sense, model, and influence neural systems for prosthetics, brain-computer interfaces, neuroimaging, and diagnostics.

Translational Tissue Engineering. Combine biology and engineering to design functional tissues and regenerative therapies.
